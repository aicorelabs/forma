How it works

Three steps.
One canvas.

No drag-and-drop. No layers. No templates. Just your assets, your words, and an AI that understands graphic design.

01
⬒
Step one
Drop your assets
Upload product images, logos, textures, or any visual material. Add inspiration images — screenshots, references, mood boards — to guide the visual direction.
02
✦
Step two
Describe the design
Tell FORMA what you need in plain language. "Bold product poster, amber palette, editorial feel." The AI reads your assets visually and generates a complete graphic design.
03
↓
Step three
Iterate & export
Refine with follow-up messages. "Make it darker." "Push the headline bigger." "Add the logo top-right." Export as HTML, copy the code, or download as a file.
